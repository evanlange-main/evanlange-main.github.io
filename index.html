FAQ

Beta questions, answered

Everything you might be wondering before you join the waitlist.

How does Spriggan’s AI investing work?
Tell Spriggan your goals, timeline, and how much risk feels comfortable. The AI designs a diversified portfolio of stocks and ETFs, funds it from your linked bank, and rebalances automatically as markets move. You stay in control the whole time — adjust your plan or place your own trades whenever you want.
When will I get access to the beta?
We’re onboarding early users in small waves so we can keep the experience smooth and listen closely to feedback. Join the waitlist and we’ll email your invite as soon as your spot opens — the earlier you sign up, the sooner you’re in.
Is my money and personal data safe?
Yes. Spriggan is built on a regulated US brokerage and uses bank-grade encryption to protect your data and identity verification (KYC). Your funds are held at the brokerage partner — not by us — and your information is never sold.
What does it cost, and do I need experience?
Joining the beta is completely free, and self-directed trades are commission-free. You don’t need any prior investing experience — let the AI handle everything for you, or take the wheel yourself. No commitments required to join the waitlist.
