FAQ

Beta questions, answered

Everything you might be wondering before you join the beta.

How does Spriggan’s automated investing work?
Tell Spriggan your goals, timeline, and how much risk feels comfortable. Spriggan’s engine designs a diversified portfolio of stocks and ETFs, funds it from your linked bank, and rebalances automatically as markets move. You stay in control the whole time — adjust your plan or place your own trades whenever you want.
How do I get access to the beta?
The beta is open to everyone — no waves, no waiting list. Sign up with your email and we’ll send your invite with download instructions right away, so you can get started the same day.
Is my money and personal data safe?
Yes. Spriggan is built on a regulated US brokerage and uses bank-grade encryption to protect your data and identity verification (KYC). Your funds are held at the brokerage partner — not by us — and your information is never sold.
What does it cost, and do I need experience?
Joining the beta is completely free, and self-directed trades are commission-free. You don’t need any prior investing experience — let the automation handle everything for you, or take the wheel yourself. No commitments required to join.
